## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, no build step (user choice, matches the sibling /Paradox/ redesign in ../PRDX). Own folder and own GitHub repo + GitHub Pages.

## Users

- Primary: aspiring barbers in the Bay Area (17+, no experience needed) deciding where to get their California barber license. Often working or in school, cost-sensitive, comparing schools.
- Secondary: licensed barbers and stylists looking for advanced education (workshops, the Core Fundamentals course, guest-educator residencies).

## Product Purpose

Sell enrollment in ĒDUCŌ Academy's 1,000-hour California barber licensing program. The site must make the program, the outcome (CA license, shop-ready skills, a shot at becoming a /Paradox/ barber), the schedule, the cost path, and the next step obvious. Workshops/classes and the Alexi Michael residency are secondary offerings for licensed pros.

## Positioning

The barber school built and run by /Paradox/, the San Jose barber collective. The curriculum was originally built to train barbers inside /Paradox/'s own shops and is used daily across their shops. Educators are working barbers. Precision-first technical training (fades, lines, graduation, layering, head structure) instead of "clocking hours". Housed inside the /Paradox/ Downtown San Jose HQ.

## Product Principles

1. Every page leads to enrollment; the next step is always one tap away.
2. Prove, don't promise: real educators, real students, real curriculum, real compliance.
3. Make the money honest and simple: tuition, scholarship, payment plans, no surprises.
4. Family, not franchise: /Paradox/ lineage is the credential, ĒDUCŌ is its own room.
