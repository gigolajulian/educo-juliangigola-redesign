We built ĒDUCŌ on one belief: the culture moves forward when knowledge is shared openly, in a room, together. IRL and inside of the academy with the intention of creating a communal space built to get better together… In person, hands on tools, face to face with the artists who've actually put work in to push the industry in a positive direction.

This is why we locked in with one of the best… to bring him back to Cali.

We didn't want to plan just another one-day seminar. We wanted something with weight to it, three days that actually change how someone builds a career, not just how they hold a shear. So we sat down with Alexi and built a mastermind: a community building showcase led by creativity on day one, the technical mastery on day two, and a branding/business masterclass to tie it all in on day three. That third day is capped on purpose. We'd rather have the right ones locked in who are serious about their growth… than fifty who are just watching. That special group will get the full VIP all-access experience of the 3-day residency.

This is personal for us. /Paradox/ was built the same way ĒDUCŌ is being built now: slow, intentional, with people we trust and a long view of what we're actually building. Alexi shares that. So does everyone who walks through our doors in November.

If you've been waiting for the right room to be in, this is it.

San Jose. November 7 to 9. This mastermind was designed with our collective and respective growth in mind.

111 N Market St, San Jose, CA 95113
