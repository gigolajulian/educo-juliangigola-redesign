Questions.

Is ĒDUCŌ licensed by the State of California?

Yes. Our academy is approved to operate by the Bureau for Private Postsecondary Education and licensed by the California Department of Consumer Affairs Board of Barbering and Cosmetology. The program is designed to meet the requirements for state licensing in barbering.

Do I need any experience?

No prior experience is necessary. The program is designed for beginners as well as those looking to enhance their skills.

How long does it take?

The 1,000-hour program takes 6–7 months full-time (Tuesday–Friday, 10:00 AM–5:00 PM) or about 1 year part-time (Monday–Thursday, 5:00 PM–9:00 PM). Both include Saturday work-from-home assignments.

How much does it cost, and is there financial aid?

Tuition for the 1,000-hour program is $20,000. We have $5,000 scholarship opportunities and flexible payment plans. We don’t currently offer federal financial aid. Contact our office for details on fees, grants, and payment options.

Will I be ready for the state board exam?

Yes. The program is designed to meet the requirements for state licensing and to have you confidently ready to take your CA state board licensing exam.

What happens after I graduate?

Graduates can pursue careers as barbers, educators, session stylists in the fashion industry, or open their own shops. We support students through our network of barbershops, career advice, and portfolio building. After the academy you will have a shot to become an official /Paradox/ barber.

Can I transfer credits from another school?

As a non-accredited institution, transfer credits are evaluated case by case. Contact admissions for details.

Can I get a cut from a student?

Yes. Students provide haircuts, beard trims, and shaves to the public under the supervision of licensed instructors, at discounted rates.
